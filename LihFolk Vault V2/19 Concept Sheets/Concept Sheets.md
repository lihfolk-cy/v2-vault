# Concept Sheets

4K (3840 x 2160) reference sheets for the [[Skeleton Library]]. Each one shows the rig from the front, the right side, the back and three-quarter, plus the full bone hierarchy, its size in studs and how to import it. Print them, pin them beside your Animation Editor, or use them as a template for your own character sheets.

## Making your own sheet (rule of thumb)
- Use orthographic front, side and back views at the same scale, so proportions can be measured, plus one three-quarter view for the feel of the model.
- Put the size in studs on the sheet. Roblox players judge scale against a 5-stud character.
- List the bone or part names exactly as they will appear in Studio, so scripters and animators use the same words.
- Keep one flat colour per part on a blockout sheet: it shows where each joint splits the body.
