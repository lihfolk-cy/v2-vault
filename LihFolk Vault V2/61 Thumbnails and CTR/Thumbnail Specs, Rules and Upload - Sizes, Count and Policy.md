## 1. Quick reference table

| Item | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail aspect ratio | **16:9**; non-16:9 images are **stretched** (not cropped) to 16:9 | Roblox docs |
| Thumbnail resolution | Ideally **1920 x 1080** | Roblox docs |
| Image formats | `.jpg`, `.gif`, `.png`, `.tga`, `.bmp` | Roblox docs |
| Max images or videos on the details page | **10** | Roblox docs |
| Thumbnails active for Home personalisation | **2 to 5** (personalisation starts at 2) | Roblox docs |
| Upload size for personalisation thumbnails | Under **3 MB** and 1920 x 1080 | Roblox docs |
| Video thumbnails | Free, **3 uploads per month**; rejected uploads still count | Roblox docs |
| Video support | Not on Xbox, PlayStation or VR headsets | Roblox docs |
| Video max length, file size, resolution | Not stated on the docs page | Unverified |
| Icon | Square, at least **512 x 512**; some surfaces show it at about **150 x 150** | Roblox docs |
| Icon file types | Not listed on the icon page; the image formats above are the safe set | Unverified |
| Icons per locale | **One** | Roblox docs |
| Experience event thumbnails | Up to **5** per event | Roblox docs |
| Sponsored ad thumbnails | Up to **10** per campaign, shown 16:9 on Home, auto-resized if not 16:9 | Roblox docs |

Sources: thumbnails https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/thumbnails · icons https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/game-icons · discovery and events https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/discovery · ads https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager

Rule of thumb on file prep: export 1920 x 1080 PNG for flat art and UI-heavy images, high-quality JPG for photographic renders, and check the file is under 3 MB before upload so the same file works for personalisation. A PNG of a busy 3D scene is often 3 to 6 MB; a JPG at quality 90 is usually under 1 MB.

## 2. Where each image shows

| Surface | Image used | Label and source |
|---|---|---|
| Home, Recommended for You | **First active 16:9 thumbnail** as a large tile (personalisation picks among active ones) | Roblox docs; staff post March 2024 https://devforum.roblox.com/t/169-experience-thumbnails-are-coming-to-home-in-april-%E2%80%93-update-yours-now/2875237 |
| Home, Continue / Favorites / Sponsored rows | **1:1 icon** | Same staff post (2024 wording; Sponsored ads now also use 16:9 creatives on Home per the ads docs) |
| Search and Discover charts | **1:1 icon** | Same staff post |
| Game details page | Thumbnails and videos at the top; an approved video appears **first** | Roblox docs, thumbnails page |
| Home, after video approval | A video can also appear on Home | Roblox docs, thumbnails page |
| Experience events | Event thumbnails (up to 5) | Roblox docs, discovery page |

The Creator Dashboard path for thumbnails is Configurations, Places, the place, **Thumbnails**, then the **Home Page** tab (personalisation set) or the **Experience Detail Page** tab (the details page gallery, where you can reorder, delete and add **alt text**). The icon path is Configurations, Places, the **start place** (starred), **Icon**, media type Image (**Roblox docs**, thumbnails and icons pages).

The Home Page tab and the Experience Detail Page tab are separate sets. A thumbnail can win on Home and still sit at position 4 on the details page; order the details page by what best confirms the promise for someone who already clicked.

### The bottom edge
Roblox advises keeping essential text or elements away from the **bottom** of a thumbnail, where metadata such as player count can cover it (**Roblox docs**, thumbnails page, and the 2024 staff post). Rule of thumb: keep critical content out of the bottom **15 to 20 %** and out of the corners, because tile chrome and rounded corners differ by platform and change over time.

## 3. Thumbnail types and the auto-generated image

- Three kinds exist: uploaded images, uploaded videos and auto-generated images; all are free (**Roblox docs**, thumbnails page).
- The auto-generated thumbnail comes from **Studio's camera position at the most recent publish** of the place (**Roblox docs**). It is not eligible for Home recommendation impressions (**Roblox docs**, discovery page), so replace it before anything else.
- On first publish, an icon is auto-generated from a set of default images (**Roblox docs**, icons page). Replace it too.

## 4. Video thumbnails

- Upload under Configurations, Places, the place, **Videos** (**Roblox docs**).
- **Allowed:** varied camera angles of real gameplay (Studio freecam helps), minor post-processing such as brightness, contrast or small effects if the core visuals stay the same, your logo or branding, non-linear highlight reels of real mechanics, in-game UI text, and music from the Roblox catalogue subject to its licensing (**Roblox docs**).
- **Not allowed:** gameplay, UI or interactions the game does not have, or a mismatched theme; visuals beyond what a player will actually see; real-life footage of people or places and other external content; voice-overs, narration, voice chat or music with lyrics; promotional or subjective claims such as discounts or "free" offers. Overlay text should be sparing and describe gameplay only (**Roblox docs**).
- Quota: **3 uploads per month**, rejections included (**Roblox docs**). Rule of thumb: review a video against the list above twice before uploading; a rejection costs a third of your month.
- Capture technique and editing are covered in [[Game Trailers and Clean Footage - Capture, Freecam and Editing]].

## 5. Policy: what keeps thumbnails approved and ranked

The thumbnails page lists video rules explicitly; for still images the docs and the Community Standards give these anchors:

- **Show what players will find.** "Use unique, relevant imagery"; ambiguous graphics confuse players (**Roblox docs**, thumbnails and icons pages).
- **Accurate metadata ranks better.** Titles that lead with monetary rewards, and metadata that does not match the content, receive less exposure in recommendations (**Roblox docs**, discovery page).
- **No deceptive or sensational metadata.** The Community Standards, under Integrity, prohibit "deceptive, sensational, duplicative, or otherwise misleading content or metadata" (https://about.roblox.com/community-standards).
- **No directing players off Roblox.** URLs of external sites or services may only be shared through the Social Links feature, and other efforts to send users off-platform are prohibited (Community Standards, Security, same URL). So: no Discord handles, web addresses or QR codes in a thumbnail.
- **No Robux schemes.** Using third-party services to buy, sell, trade or give away Robux is prohibited (Community Standards, Roblox Economy). Rule of thumb: never show "FREE ROBUX", Robux piles, or real-money prices on art.
- **Moderation applies to everything.** Uploads are moderated against the Community Rules and Terms of Use; every icon must pass moderation before others see it (**Roblox docs**).
- **Audience and maturity.** Imagery must fit your experience's maturity label; a thumbnail more intense than the label allows is a moderation and trust problem (see [[Roblox Policies Ratings and Compliance]]).
- **AI-made art** follows the same rules; see [[Roblox Policies on AI-Generated Content]] and [[AI Image Generation for Icons Thumbnails and Concepts]].

Practical reading (Rule of thumb): a still thumbnail may be a staged, well-lit, posed shot of real content with tasteful grading and a logo. It should not contain characters, items, effects, UI or places that are not in the game, and should not look like a different art style from the game players load into.

## 6. Localisation

- **Icons:** you can display **one icon per locale** (**Roblox docs**, icons page). Localised icons are managed with your localisation settings (Creator Dashboard, the experience, Audience, Localization: https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/localization).
- **Thumbnails:** the docs thumbnails page does not describe localised thumbnails. A 2024 staff post said manual icon and thumbnail translations take priority over generated ones, and announced a two-week test of **automatic translation of text inside icons and thumbnails** for French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Polish, Portuguese and Spanish, with a planned on/off setting afterwards (https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-icon-thumbnail-translation-experiment/3105942). Whether that auto-translation is live for all games today, and how localised thumbnails interact with personalisation, is **Unverified**.
- Rule of thumb: design thumbnails that need **no text**, so one set works in every market; put any language-specific words on a separate variant you can localise or retire.
- Game names and descriptions can be auto-translated for players with the Automatic Translations setting on (**Roblox docs**, localisation page), so a clean, literal title translates better than slang or puns. See [[Localization]].

## 7. Accessibility

- Add **alt text** to details-page thumbnails (**Roblox docs**, thumbnails page): one sentence describing what the image shows ("Avatar running from a giant tidal wave toward a glowing egg").
- Value contrast that reads in greyscale helps colour-blind players and small screens alike ([[Accessibility Contrast]], [[Thumbnail Contrast]]).

## Common mistakes

- Avoid: uploading 4:3 or square art as a thumbnail. Symptom: the image is stretched, characters look fat.
- Avoid: leaving the auto-generated thumbnail. Symptom: zero Home recommendation impressions.
- Avoid: text along the bottom edge. Symptom: the player count covers your hook.
- Avoid: off-platform handles, QR codes or Robux claims on art. Symptom: moderation rejection or removal.
- Avoid: uploading a video with lyric music or voice. Symptom: rejection, and one of three monthly uploads gone.
- Avoid: PNG renders over 3 MB. Symptom: the upload fails in the personalisation flow.
- Avoid: a seasonal thumbnail still active months later. Symptom: players read the page as abandoned.

## Checklist

- [ ] All thumbnails 16:9, 1920 x 1080, under 3 MB.
- [ ] 2 to 5 active on the Home Page tab; details page ordered and alt-texted separately.
- [ ] Icon square, at least 512 x 512, previewed at 150 x 150.
- [ ] Nothing critical in the bottom 15 to 20 % or the corners.
- [ ] No off-platform links, Robux claims, fake UI or content not in the game.
- [ ] Videos checked against the allowed and not-allowed lists before upload.
- [ ] Icon localised for top locales; thumbnails text-free or with localisable variants.
- [ ] Specs re-checked against the docs pages above before each major refresh.
