# Community and Moderation Start Here

This section is about the people around your game: the players who talk to each other in it, the staff who keep it safe, the creators who film it, and the off-platform spaces where your most engaged fans gather. On Roblox those topics cannot be separated from child safety. Most players are young, many cannot chat at all, and the platform's rules on communication changed more in 2026 than in the previous five years: age checks for chat, external links removed from games, social links limited to age-checked 16+ viewers, and, announced in October 2026, platform-integrated chat that takes message text out of your code's reach by 2027.

Every note here therefore opens with a "Safety first" section built on Roblox's Community Standards before it gets to features. If you only read one thing, read that section in each note.

## Five principles that run through every note

1. **Roblox owns safety; you own your game's rules.** Anything involving a child's safety goes to Roblox's report system, not to a volunteer moderator's DMs.
2. **Your systems may only narrow communication, never widen it.** Proximity, team and party rules are fine; anything that lets strangers talk privately, or moves players off-platform, is not.
3. **Never read, log or store what players say.** It is personal data, and from 2027 the chat API will not give it to you anyway.
4. **Design for the player who cannot chat or speak.** Pings, emotes, quick phrases and clear UI are mandatory, not extras.
5. **Every staff power is server-checked and logged.** Moderators are trusted, verified, and accountable.

## Time-sensitive facts

This section was written against Roblox docs and announcements as of October 2026. The parts most likely to change: platform-integrated chat dates (opt-in now, default February 2027, required May 2027), voice eligibility rules, Safety Dashboard thresholds, and Discord's teen-by-default settings.
