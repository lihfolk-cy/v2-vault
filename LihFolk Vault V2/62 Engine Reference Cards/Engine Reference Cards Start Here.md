# Engine Reference Cards Start Here

One compact card per engine class: the members you reach for most, their defaults and limits, who may change them, the usual mistakes and a short checklist. Every fact on a card was checked against the class page on create.roblox.com on the date in its Source line. For deeper explanations, follow the topic links at the top of each card, or browse [[API and Concept Atlas Start Here]].

## How to use a card

- Scan **Members that matter** for the exact name, signature and whether it yields.
- Check **Limits and defaults** before hard-coding a number.
- Read **Replication and authority** before deciding which side runs the code.
