# Business and Careers Start Here

> **Not advice.** Everything in this section is general information. It is not tax, legal or financial advice. Tax, contract, employment and copyright rules differ by country, and often by state or province. The binding platform rules are Roblox's own: the Terms of Use, Community Standards, Creator Store terms and the **Developer Exchange Terms of Use** with the DevEx page on the Creator Hub. Read the current versions before acting, and ask a qualified professional for anything large. **If you are under 18**, a parent or guardian needs to be part of any contract, payout, bank account or tax setup.

This section covers the work around making games: getting paid, paying others, protecting what you make, and building a career or a studio. The notes assume Roblox realities: young developers, payment in Robux, games owned by groups, and rules that change.

## Five ideas that run through every note

1. **Write it down.** Most disputes in Roblox teams come from verbal deals: scope, shares, ownership, exits. A short written agreement both sides accept prevents most of them.
2. **Own through a group, with least privilege.** Games and assets belong to a group; people get only the roles they need ([[Groups Roles and Permissions for Studios]], [[Animation Asset Permissions and Groups]]).
3. **Plan in Earned Robux, convert late.** The DevEx rate is set by Roblox and has changed. Shares written in Earned Robux age better than promises in dollars.
4. **Money arrives late and varies.** Budget from averages, keep reserves, and do not treat one good month as a salary.
5. **Safety first, especially for minors.** Keep work communication in visible, moderated channels, never ask for personal information, follow Roblox's Community Standards, and involve parents or guardians in anything involving money or agreements.
