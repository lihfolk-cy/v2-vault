## In short

Sightlines are what the player actually sees from a spot; they also leak locked zones, the map edge and unfinished areas (from [[Level Design Principles - Flow Sightlines and Landmarks]]).

## Key facts

- If your game supports portrait mode, horizontal view collapses to about a third and almost every lateral sightline breaks. ([[Level Design Principles - Flow Sightlines and Landmarks]])
- Sightline parity. Every long sightline should have a matching one. ([[Combat Arena and Encounter Layout]])
- Sightline length should match the roster: no 400-stud sightlines if there are no long-range weapons. ([[Genre Playbook - Shooters FPS and TPS]])
- The objective is the landmark. Light it, colour it in team-neutral accents, and make it visible from each spawn exit (Level Design Principles - Flow Sightlines and Landmarks). ([[Objective Mode Maps - Capture the Flag, King of the Hill and Control Points]])
