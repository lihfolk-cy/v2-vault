## In short

Lore is the history, rules and mysteries that make a world feel like it existed before the player arrived. (from [[Lore Delivery Without Walls of Text]])

## Key facts

- Avoid: Opening lore crawl -> Show the world; put history in optional layers. ([[Lore Delivery Without Walls of Text]])
- If you use real names, the tier colour, the frame and the item card must carry the rarity, and the lore line can carry the history. ([[Naming Items Zones and Abilities]])
- Proper-noun-heavy lore. A codex entry full of invented names will translate oddly no matter what. ([[Localization-Ready Writing]])
- Well over a hundred weapons, each with a rigged model, an icon, lore, a voice, and (for higher rarities) a full attack kit of animated moves and an ultimate. ([[Case Study - Building a Game with AI Agents]])
