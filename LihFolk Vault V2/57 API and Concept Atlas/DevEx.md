## In short

DevEx is requested by a person, so group revenue reaches a person through group payouts first (Scoping and Roles for Small Teams covers one-time and recurring payouts). (from [[DevEx and Cashing Out]])

## Key facts

- Read the current DevEx Terms of Use and the DevEx page on the Creator Hub. ([[DevEx and Cashing Out]])
