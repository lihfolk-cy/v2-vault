## In short

Edge flow is the direction those loops travel across the surface, and for anything that deforms it should follow the way the surface moves. (from [[Topology and Edge Flow]])

## Key facts

- Mouth line (the edge between lips or jaw plates) should be a clean loop so the jaw can open without the face tearing; see Topology and Edge Flow. ([[Organic and Creature Modeling]])
- Even edge flow around joints. The garment deforms by skinning plus cage fitting; a shoulder with two edge loops creases badly in arms-up poses. ([[Layered Clothing Creation Workflow]])
- 1,248 triangles for a whole leg segment set is tight: limbs must be simple tubes with good joint loops (Topology and Edge Flow). ([[Modeling Characters for R15 and Custom Rigs]])
- Joints that deform. Elbows, knees and shoulders need loops for clean bending; see Topology and Edge Flow. ([[Spending Triangles Where the Camera Looks]])
