## In short

A paid random item is a random outcome bought directly or indirectly with Robux, including with in-game currency that can be bought with Robux. (from [[Paid Random Items and Odds Disclosure in Practice]])

## Key facts

- Every paid random item: all outcomes, numerical odds, 100% sum, labelled details button. ([[Roblox Policies Ratings and Compliance]])
- Every paid random item and luck modifier shows numeric odds summing to 100% before purchase. ([[Policy and Age Rules 2026]])
- Any paid random item shows numerical odds for every outcome before purchase. ([[Roblox Changes 2025-2026]])
