## In short

Decimate only what remains over budget. (from [[Retopology and Decimation]])

## Key facts

- Avoid: Remeshing a UV-mapped model -> Decimate, or redo UVs. ([[Retopology and Decimation]])
- Avoid: Decimate without symmetry -> Symmetry on. ([[Retopology and Decimation]])
- Avoid: Treating decimation output as final topology. ([[Procedural Creatures - Fused Shells, Quad Cages and Faceting]])
- Avoid: Shipping a ZBrush or scan mesh directly -> Retopo or decimate to budget. ([[Other Modeling Tools - Maya Max ZBrush MagicaVoxel]])
- Avoid: One mesh over 20,000 triangles -> Split by material or body part, or decimate. ([[Modeling Fundamentals for Games]])
