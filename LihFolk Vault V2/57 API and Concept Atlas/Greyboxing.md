## In short

A blockout is the level built from grey Parts with no art. (from [[Level Design for Roblox]])

## Key facts

- Avoid: Building art before the blockout is fun -> Blockout and playtest first. ([[Level Design for Roblox]])
- Blockout playtested on PC and phone before art. ([[Level Design for Roblox]])
- Blockout in Parts with names and pivots that the final meshes can map onto. ([[Parts Unions and MeshParts]])
- Blockout measured in place, numbers written down. ([[Art Pipeline - Concept to In-Game Asset]])
- Blockout at final scale beside a character proxy, checked in Studio. ([[Modeling Fundamentals for Games]])
