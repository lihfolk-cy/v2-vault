## In short

Roblox thumbnails are seen small, on phones, next to dozens of others, by young players, and are read in under a second (from [[Thumbnails Icons and Store Page]]).

## Key facts

- Avoid: uploading 4:3 or square art as a thumbnail. ([[Thumbnail Specs, Rules and Upload - Sizes, Count and Policy]])
- Avoid: leaving the auto-generated thumbnail. ([[Thumbnail Specs, Rules and Upload - Sizes, Count and Policy]])
- Avoid: Shipping with the auto-generated thumbnail (not eligible for Home recommendations). ([[Thumbnails Icons and Store Page]])
- Avoid: Collage thumbnails with ten features at tile size. ([[Thumbnails Icons and Store Page]])
- Avoid: Illustrated art that promises a look the game does not have; allowed for images, not for video thumbnails, and costly in... ([[Cross-Game Patterns - Thumbnails Icons and Titles That Get Clicks]])
- Thumbnails share one template; key text is clear of the bottom edge. ([[Cross-Game Patterns - Thumbnails Icons and Titles That Get Clicks]])
