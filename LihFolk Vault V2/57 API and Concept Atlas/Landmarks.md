## In short

A landmark that gives one side a long view is a balance bug. (from [[Level Design Principles - Flow Sightlines and Landmarks]])

## Key facts

- Avoid: Landmarks too small -> Raise them, move them closer, or add a far stand-in. ([[Level Design Principles - Flow Sightlines and Landmarks]])
- One-page brief per zone with identity sentence, three reads, landmark and "not this" list. ([[Zone and Biome Art Direction]])
- Bevels visible in Blender, invisible in game: Width under 2 pixels at play distance -> Size from the distance table; double for landmarks. ([[Bevels Chamfers and Edge Highlights Without Textures]])
- Each zone has a surface identity (name, landmark, one line) that works with no reading. ([[Lore Delivery Without Walls of Text]])
- Ground roles defined: path, play space, edges, hazards, landmarks. ([[Terrain Materials and Painting]])
