## In short

`Rebirths` is an `IntValue` and comes first, so sorting is numeric and meaningful. (from [[Rebirth and Prestige System End to End]])

Rebirth is one non-yielding transaction with clear NOW/AFTER UI. (from [[Progression Levels and Unlocks]])

## Key facts

- Avoid: Rebirth cost growth with no second layer. ([[Rebirth and Prestige System End to End]])
- Avoid: Linear rebirth multiplier against exponential rebirth cost -> Geometric multiplier slightly below the cost growth. ([[Progression and Pacing Design]])
- Rebirth time per rebirth rises gently, with new multiplier sources planned. ([[Progression and Pacing Design]])
- Rebirths unlock features rather than only resetting numbers. ([[Case Study - Pet Simulator 99]])
- Walls are a design choice. A deliberate steepening before a rebirth or a new zone tells the player "change strategy". ([[Power Curve]])
