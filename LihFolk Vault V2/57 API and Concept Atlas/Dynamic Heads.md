## In short

Dynamic heads are skinned head meshes driven by a standard set of FACS controls through a `FaceControls` instance; they work with the Animation Editor's face tools, camera-based capture and avatar systems. (from [[Facial Animation and Dynamic Heads]])

Dynamic head: A skinned head driven by FACS controls through FaceControls. (from [[Animation Glossary]])

## Key facts

- Heads mean FACS. A dynamic head needs face controls and expressions; budget animation time, not just modelling (Facial Animation and Dynamic Heads). ([[Bundles Animation Packs and Emotes for Sale]])
- Facial keys drive the head's FACS controls; see Facial Animation and Dynamic Heads. ([[Animation Capture and Mocap]])
- `FaceControls`: about 50 numeric FACS properties on dynamic heads, animated through FloatCurves or NumberPoses (see Facial Animation and Dynamic Heads). ([[Animation Objects]])
- Add `expression` per node and drive a dynamic head or a portrait sheet (Facial Animation and Dynamic Heads). ([[Dialogue System End to End]])
