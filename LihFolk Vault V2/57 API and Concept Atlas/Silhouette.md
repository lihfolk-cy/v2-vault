## In short

Silhouette is the cheapest to vary (reshape and re-scale props from the same kit), and light is the most powerful per hour of work (a lighting profile is minutes of tuning and changes everything on screen). (from [[Zone and Biome Art Direction]])

## Key facts

- Primary, secondary and tertiary shapes identified; tertiaries under about 10 to 15 percent of the silhouette. ([[Shape Language and Silhouette Testing]])
- Budget met but model looks plain: Triangles spread evenly, none on silhouette or focal feature -> Move triangles to the top silhouette and one focal feature. ([[Spending Triangles Where the Camera Looks]])
- Avoid: Weapon inside the silhouette -> move the weapon outside the body outline. ([[Posing and Silhouettes]])
- Same problems come back every round: Feedback on detail before silhouette -> Critique in the fixed order; stop at the first failing level. ([[Critiquing a Model - Paintovers and Iteration]])
- Critique in order: purpose, silhouette, proportion, value, colour, edges, detail, technical. ([[Critiquing a Model - Paintovers and Iteration]])
