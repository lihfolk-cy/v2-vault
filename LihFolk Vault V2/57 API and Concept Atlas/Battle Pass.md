## In short

A battle pass (Roblox's docs call it a season pass) is a time-limited progression track: players earn season XP from missions, climb tiers, and claim rewards before the season ends. (from [[Battle Pass and Season Design]])

## Key facts

- Earn XP and battle pass progress; return to the lobby. ([[Teardown - BedWars]])
- Battle pass and gacha: the October 2026 description mentions a limited battle pass, a gacha and a skin crate with animated rods. ([[Teardown - Fish It]])
- Crates and a battle pass add recurring revenue; any paid crate needs disclosed odds and `PolicyService` gating (Paid Random Items and Odds Disclosure in Practice). ([[Case Study - Tower Defense Simulator]])
- A season end and a season start never share a day: the docs recommend a rest week between seasons (Battle Pass and Season Design). ([[Event Calendars and Content Planning]])
