## In short

Layered clothing is Roblox's system for 3D garments that fit any body and stack over each other (a shirt under a jacket under a scarf). (from [[Layered Clothing Cages and Animation]])

Layered clothing uses the same names to position, then cages to fit. (from [[R15 Rig Anatomy and Attachments]])

## Key facts

- Emote tested on several bodies and with bulky layered clothing. ([[Bundles Animation Packs and Emotes for Sale]])
- Roblox supports skinned meshes driven by `Bone` instances, used for creatures, custom characters, layered clothing and soft props. ([[Skinning Weights]])
- With layered clothing the opposite holds: puffy jackets and dresses clip more than the dummy predicts. ([[Layered Clothing Cages and Animation]])
- Layered clothing stretches to fit, but extreme proportions expose thin areas (armpits, crotch, collar). ([[Layered Clothing Creation Workflow]])
