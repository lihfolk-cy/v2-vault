## In short

Active Spender: A paying user, the unit of Creator Rewards payouts. (from [[Glossary]])

## Key facts

- Avoid: Ignoring Creator Rewards in LTV, or counting them for users who were not attributed. ([[Unit Economics - ARPDAU, Payer Conversion, LTV and Ad Payback]])
- Creator Rewards: platform payouts for engaging and bringing in spending users, which replaced Engagement-Based Payouts ("Premium Payouts") on July 24, 2025. ([[Subscriptions and Premium Payouts]])
- The old Creator Affiliate program (affiliate share links paying up to 50% of a new user's Robux purchases) was deprecated in July 2025 and replaced by Creator Rewards (Roblox docs). ([[Working with Content Creators]])
- Creator Rewards and Plus bonuses have a 60-day hold (Docs). ([[Monetization Overview]])
