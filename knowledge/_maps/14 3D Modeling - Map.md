- Hard-Surface Modeling: Avoid: Bevels on unapplied scale -> Apply scale first
- Low-Poly and Stylized Modeling: Avoid: Timid proportions -> Exaggerate scale contrast and one feature
- Mesh Optimization for Mobile: FPS dies looking at foliage: Overlapping alpha cards, `DoubleSided` transparency -> Geometry clumps, opaque styles, fewer overlaps
