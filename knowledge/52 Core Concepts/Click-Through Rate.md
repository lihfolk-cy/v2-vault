# Click-Through Rate

**Click-through rate (CTR)** is the share of people who saw something and clicked it: clicks divided by impressions. On Roblox it most often means how many players who saw your experience's tile in discovery (home sort, search, recommendations) opened or played it. It also applies to ads, in-game prompts and shop buttons.

## Why it matters on Roblox

Discovery is a competition for attention on a small tile: an icon or thumbnail, a title and a few stats. If few viewers click, the game gets few players no matter how good it is, and discovery systems that see low engagement from impressions are less likely to keep showing it. The Creator Dashboard reports impressions and plays from discovery surfaces, and Roblox has offered thumbnail experiments to compare images (Roblox docs, as of October 2026; exact metric names and experiment features change, so check your dashboard; not verified in detail for this note).

## The nuance most people miss

1. **CTR is only half the funnel.** A misleading thumbnail can raise CTR and then lose players in the first minute, which hurts more than it helps. Track clicks together with early retention from the same source.
2. **CTR depends on placement.** The same icon gets a different CTR in search (players looking for it) than in a recommendation row (players browsing). Compare like with like.
3. **Small differences need large samples.** A change from 4.0 to 4.3 percent needs many thousands of impressions per variant before it means anything. See [[Variance]].
4. **Test one thing at a time.** Changing the icon, title and thumbnail together tells you nothing about which change worked.
5. **CTR decays with familiarity.** Players who have already seen your tile many times click less. Fresh art for an update can lift CTR even if the old art was fine.
6. **In-game CTR matters too.** The share of players who open the shop after seeing its button, or who accept a prompt, is the same metric. Log impressions (button shown) as well as clicks.

## Tiny example

| Icon | Impressions | Plays | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: character close-up, bright background | 40,000 | 1,720 | 4.3% |
| B: wide scene, small character | 40,000 | 1,280 | 3.2% |

A wins on CTR. Before switching, check that day-1 retention from A's players is not lower.
